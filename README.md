# Student Performance Visualization

## Objective
Visualize student performance data using different charts and identify trends.

## Visualizations

### 1. Final Grade Distribution
The histogram shows the distribution of final grades among students.

### 2. Gender Distribution
The dataset contains an equal number of male and female students.

### 3. Study Hours vs Final Grade
A positive relationship is visible between study hours and final grades.

### 4. Attendance vs Final Grade
Students with higher attendance tend to achieve better grades.

### 5. Correlation Heatmap
Attendance, study hours, and previous grades show strong positive correlation with final grades.

## Conclusion
Student performance is strongly influenced by attendance, study hours, and previous academic performance.
